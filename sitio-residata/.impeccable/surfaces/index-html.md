Scope: whole marketing site (index, producto, planes, novedades, soporte, contacto, legal). Visitor mode: Persuade (novedades and legal pages: Read).
Audience: heads of services at Bolivian universities, municipalities and large-campus companies evaluating a waste-collection platform; secondary: current clients (support, releases) and investors.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every kilo weighed is a receipt. The site is printed as the thermal ticket a scale issues; kg figures carry the hierarchy. Refuses the split hero with a dashboard mockup and a row of feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: White thermal paper (#fbfbf8) strips on a graphite counter (#17191c); dot-matrix monospaced figures for every number, a condensed grotesque for headings; perforated tear lines between sections; dashed rules, right-aligned kg columns, barcode/QR footers; green verification stamp (#0b6b4f) for confirmed states, red (#d4351c) only for alerts and the primary action.
STORY: The visitor sees a real-looking weighing ticket, understands Residata turns each emptying into a kilogram record, sees the product annotated with leader lines, scrubs the academic calendar forecast, reads plans in Bs, and requests a demo.
